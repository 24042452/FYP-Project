## Scope

Design mockups (planning phase, static HTML/CSS/JS in `design/`, not product code): Home (desktop + phone), Login, Check-up, Transactions, NETS Assistant. Mode: Operate. Audiences: hawker owner on phone and multi-outlet owner/accountant on desktop, equally; staff roles (Owner, Manager, Cashier, Accountant) visible. Keep: answer-first headline, plain word with technical term underneath, menu groups My business / My money / Grow / Settings; unbuilt modules greyed out with "Soon". Avoid: too dense/techy, too playful, themed metaphors.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A conventional merchant portal at Stripe Dashboard calibre, made plain: answer first, plain words, one main action per screen.
OWN-WORLD: Light. Page #F5F7FA, white panels, 1px #E2E7EE hairlines, navy ink #14213D; blue #0B4DB8 for data and navigation, red #D3162B for brand mark, main action and problems; status pills always icon + word + colour; Inter with tabular numerals; 12px panels, 8px controls, quiet shadows.
STORY: Owners see how business is going, what reaches the bank, what needs attention; accountants filter, check and export.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Grouped left sidebar with outlet switcher and Ask button; top bar with search, date range, alerts, user and role; headline sentence; one summary strip of three figures; sales chart beside channel mix; primary action "See what needs attention".
FORM: canon (standing exit, user-chosen), reference Stripe Dashboard; seed f133241d.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
