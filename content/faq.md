# Sample help content, not official NETS policy

These answers are written for the prototype so the assistant has something to look up. They are not official NETS policy. Each `#` heading is a topic; each `##` heading is a question.

# Settlements

## When will I get paid?
NETS pays your sales into your bank account about 1 to 2 business days after the sale. Card and NETS QR sales are usually paid the next business day. Weekend and public holiday sales are paid on the next business day. You can see each payout, and when it is due, on the Money to my bank page.

## Why is my money late?
Money is late when it has not reached your bank by the date it was due. Most often this is a bank holiday, a bank processing delay, or a wrong bank account number on file. Check the Money to my bank page for anything marked Late. If it is more than 2 business days late, ask NETS to check it from the Requests page.

## What does pending, paid and delayed mean?
Pending means NETS has your sales and the money is on its way. Paid means it has reached your bank. Delayed means NETS has flagged a problem with that payout and is looking into it.

## Why is my payout smaller than my sales?
Each payout is your sales minus the NETS fees for that day (the MDR). So the amount in your bank is lower than the sales total. The Home page shows where your money went: sales, NETS fees, and what goes to your bank.

# Payments

## Why do card payments get declined?
A card is usually declined by the customer's bank, not by NETS or your terminal. The common reasons are not enough funds, a card limit, or the bank being briefly unavailable. Ask the customer to try another card or pay another way. The customer is not charged for a declined payment.

## What do the payment failure codes mean?
Code 51 means the customer's bank declined it for not enough funds. Code 05 means the bank declined it for another reason. Code 91 means the bank system was briefly unavailable, so try again in a minute. TO means the payment timed out before the customer confirmed. CX means the customer cancelled.

## What should I do when a payment fails?
Ask the customer to try again or pay another way, for example NETS QR instead of a card. If the same terminal keeps failing, contact NETS support. Nothing is charged to the customer when a payment fails.

## Will a failed payment be charged to the customer?
No. A failed payment does not take money from the customer and does not appear in your payout. Only payments marked Went through are paid out.

## How do I find a payment?
Go to Sales & payments and search by amount, such as 12.50, or by the start of the payment ID. You can also filter by date, payment type and status.

# Fees

## What is MDR?
MDR stands for Merchant Discount Rate. It is the fee NETS charges as a percentage of each sale, taken from your payout. The rate depends on the payment type, such as NETS QR, cards or eNETS online. Your bill lists the MDR for each payment type.

## What is GST on my bill?
GST is the 9 percent goods and services tax charged on NETS fees if your business is GST-registered. It is shown as a separate line on your bill. If you are not GST-registered, no GST is added.

## How do NETS fees work?
You pay three kinds of charges: the MDR on each sale, a monthly terminal rental for each terminal, and a small portal fee. The MDR is taken from each payout. Rental and the portal fee are on your monthly bill.

# Bills

## What are the charges on my bill?
Your monthly bill has the MDR for each payment type, terminal rental for each terminal, and a portal fee, plus GST if you are registered. Open Bills & charges and tap a bill to see every line.

## When is my bill due?
Each monthly bill is due about 14 days after the month ends. The due date is shown on the bill, and a bill still unpaid after that date is marked Overdue.

## How do I download an invoice?
Open Bills & charges, pick the month, and choose Download PDF. You can send this to your accountant.

# Check-up

## What is the check-up?
The check-up compares your sales records with what NETS paid to your bank and what it billed you. It flags anything that does not match, such as a missing payout or a wrong fee, so you can ask NETS to fix it.

## How do I export for my accountant?
Open Check-up and choose Download Excel for the month. The file lists every payment, payout and fee in plain columns. You can also download a PDF summary of the check-up.

# Account

## How do I contact NETS?
Use the Requests page to send NETS a message about a payout, a bill or a terminal. For urgent terminal problems, call the NETS merchant helpline shown on your terminal sticker.

## How do I change my bank account?
Changing the bank account that receives your money is done through a request, for security. Go to the Requests page and start a bank account change. NETS will check it with you before anything changes. The assistant cannot change it for you.

## How do I add or remove a terminal?
Terminal changes are made through the Requests page. NETS will arrange delivery or collection. The assistant cannot change terminals for you.
